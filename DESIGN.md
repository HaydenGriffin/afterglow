# TV listening surface

Experience first; controls appear on OK and recede after nine seconds. The
existing visualizer remains the visual authority. Album and lyrics modes extend
it using the inspected Music Assistant full-screen composition, adapted to a
sofa and directional remote. Dark surfaces keep contrast over arbitrary presets.

Use the existing geometric sans-serif stack, warm white text, warm neutral focus
rings, generous safe areas and large song titles. Album artwork is content,
requested at 512 px; no extra blur filters or animated background layers.

Four views: Visuals only; Visuals + track (existing timed/always card); Album;
Lyrics. Artwork/lyrics default to a calm background with renderer drawing stopped.
Optional MilkDrop background and timed lyrics overlay are explicit preferences.

All controls work with arrows/OK/Back and pointer. Back closes a panel before
leaving the app. Stable native buttons, visible focus, disabled/loading/error
states, bounded up-next list and lyric document; no new front-end framework.
